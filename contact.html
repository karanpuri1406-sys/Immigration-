After you send it

What happens next

  1. We read it properly

    A person reads what you have sent and looks at your actual circumstances. It is not an automated reply.

  2. We come back with questions

    Almost always. Occupation coding, English results and dates decide most cases, and details matter.

  3. You get an assessment

    Which subclasses are open to you, which are not, and what would need to change. Free, and yours to keep.

  4. You decide

    If you want to proceed, you get costs and timeframes in writing first. If you do not, that is the end of it.
